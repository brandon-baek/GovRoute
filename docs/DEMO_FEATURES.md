# GovRoute: features worth demonstrating

These are product behaviors to show on screen, not claims of universal legal coverage.

## A practical three-minute story

1. **Ask about a local task at a real address.** Confirm the geographic match and show the responsible local procedure, official-page instructions, and source links. For LA County, explicitly confirm a parcel and show its zoning evidence. Explain that geocoder points and candidate parcels have limits.
2. **Turn guidance into a plan.** Open a passport or state/local guide and choose Add to my action plan. Add an interstate moving checklist as another task. Open My action plan and show several agencies represented in one workspace.
3. **Show the next action changing.** Complete a prerequisite and show dependent actions become ready. Undo it and show dependent completion is reset. This demonstrates a real dependency model rather than a static list.
4. **Prepare and take the plan with you.** Open the preparation packet, show that exact repeated requirement wording is consolidated with its agency uses, choose a personal milestone, and export the .ics calendar file. Export/import the plan JSON to demonstrate portability without an account.
5. **Show an evidence receipt.** Recheck saved guides and inspect the retained source links, saved date and current recorded review status. A changed published guide is flagged by section, and replacing it resets affected completion. A successful recheck is not a claim that every government webpage was checked live.

## Accurate feature descriptions

- Address-aware local routing and official-source discovery: geographic context, task scope and source evidence determine what is shown.
- Multi-agency action plans: combine published guides and cross-state moving tasks into one browser-local workspace.
- Dependency-aware next actions: waiting steps unlock after their prerequisites; undoing a prerequisite clears dependent completion.
- Preparation packets: exact repeated requirement wording is combined. The app does not claim that one document is automatically accepted by every agency.
- Personal milestone calendar: exports all-day target dates chosen by the user, with source notes. It does not calculate legal deadlines or create automatic reminders.
- Saved-guide evidence receipts: compare the saved instruction snapshot with GovRoute's current published guide and recorded review status. This is not a live legal audit.
- Portable private plans: browser storage plus validated JSON export/import; no account, identity-document upload, personal-plan database or paid AI call is needed.

## Boundaries to explain candidly

Detailed parcel/zoning connectors currently cover Los Angeles County. Local-source discovery depends on linked official sources, and some jurisdictions/tasks have coverage gaps. The moving planner has detailed checked destination guidance for a limited set of states and labels the remaining destinations accordingly. Imported saved references need a source-version recheck before acting. Never describe these limits as completed nationwide legal coverage.

The student should explain their own contributions and technical understanding and disclose AI assistance as required by the competition rules.
