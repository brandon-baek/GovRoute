This public government dataset is a tested serving projection of the national Census, USGS geographic-name, and CISA .gov registrant imports.

The full geographic archive is published as a separate compressed release asset and is also included in the associated Actions artifact. The serving projection retains IDs, official and unofficial names, geographical relationships, government status, reviewed topic scopes, publications, directory provenance, and machine-indexed local pages. It removes raw feature attributes except the layer required by address resolution.

The accompanying manifest records the uncompressed SHA-256, schema version, byte budgets, generation time, and measured place and procedure counts. Website builds verify this manifest and download the immutable release asset. Functions bundle its compressed form and extract it through a bounded stream into ephemeral storage during a shared initialization. Failed imports, oversized traces, or failed user scenarios cannot advance the website's serving manifest.

Place records and registered websites do not prove full procedure coverage. Only separately reviewed process publications may supply verified steps. Local pages and registrations remain labeled as unreviewed starting points. Failed or blocked crawls remain in the coverage report.
